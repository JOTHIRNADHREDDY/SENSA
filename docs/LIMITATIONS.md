# Limitations

- The approved investigation uses synthetic recordings. It verifies traceability and workflow, without establishing real-world theft detection accuracy.
- Anonymous associations can fragment or confuse nearby objects. Camera 1’s overlapping proximity episodes retain seven alternative object-track candidates. Physical continuity remains unverified.
- Fixture clock origins are verified only for the generated recordings. Real camera synchronization needs independent verification.
- Discovery is sampled and bounded. Unsearched ranges remain explicit and are not negative evidence.
- The accepted shelf comparison shows 6 to 5 visible detections and a separate synthetic reference delta of −1. It remains unresolved, with no confirmed removal or human conclusion.
- Occlusion, query coverage, detector errors, scene cuts and unsupported alignment can make comparisons unavailable.
- Public review exposes approved synthetic evidence only. The full saved workspace database and raw model payloads remain private.
- Docker and live HTTPS checks require a Docker host and an owner-controlled domain. Local execution does not establish public deployment.
- One local SQLite volume is supported. Network filesystem SQLite, multiple API replicas, and multi-host storage are outside the validated deployment.
- Source hashes and the audit chain help detect changes. They do not prove recording authenticity or establish legal admissibility.
